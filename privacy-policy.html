Privacy Policy

Golden Grace Cleaning And Gardening Solutions · Client Privacy & Data Protection

Golden Grace Cleaning And Gardening Solutions respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, manage and disclose personal information when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, request a quotation, make a booking, communicate with us or use our cleaning, gardening or other services.

We aim to manage personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) and applicable Australian privacy laws, including the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), where those laws apply to our business or relevant activities.

Golden Grace Cleaning And Gardening Solutions is a registered business name operated by Divine Grace Business Group Pty Ltd.

2. Personal Information We Collect

The type of personal information we collect depends on how you interact with us and the services you request.

We may collect the following categories of information.

Personal and Contact Information

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Telephone or mobile number.
  • Residential or business address.
  • Service location, suburb and postcode.
  • Business or organisation name.
  • Contact details of an authorised representative, property manager or other person arranging a service.

Quotation and Booking Information

  • Requested cleaning or gardening services.
  • Property type and size.
  • Number of rooms, bathrooms or other relevant areas.
  • Preferred service dates and times.
  • Service frequency, such as weekly, fortnightly or one-off.
  • Additional services and special requirements.
  • Access instructions, parking information and relevant site requirements.
  • Information about the condition of the property and areas requiring attention.

Commercial and Business Information

For commercial cleaning and property maintenance enquiries, we may collect:

  • Company or organisation name.
  • Business contact details.
  • Workplace or facility type.
  • Approximate floor area and relevant facilities.
  • Cleaning schedules and service requirements.
  • Site access conditions and operational instructions.
  • Information required to prepare a quotation or service agreement.

Photographs and Documents

Customers may voluntarily provide photographs, videos or documents to help us assess properties, prepare quotations, understand the required work or manage a booking.

These materials may include images of rooms, carpets, windows, gardens, buildings or other areas where services are requested.

Please avoid submitting unnecessary personal, financial, medical or other sensitive information through our website forms.

Payment and Transaction Information

We may collect information relating to quotations, invoices, payments, deposits, outstanding balances and transaction records.

We do not request complete payment-card details through ordinary enquiry forms. Where an electronic payment service is offered, the relevant payment provider may process payment information in accordance with its own privacy and security practices.

Technical and Website Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, depending on the website systems and tools in use.

This may include:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Website pages visited.
  • Date and time of visits.
  • Referring website information.
  • Cookie and website usage information.
  • General interactions with website features.

3. How We Collect Personal Information

We generally collect personal information directly from you when you:

  • Complete a contact or quotation request form on our website.
  • Contact us by telephone, email or other messaging services.
  • Request or accept a quotation.
  • Make or manage a booking.
  • Communicate with our representatives or cleaning team.
  • Provide photographs, documents or other information.
  • Submit feedback, reviews or complaints.
  • Interact with our social media pages.

In some circumstances, we may receive information from a person arranging a service on your behalf, such as a property owner, real estate agent, property manager, business representative or authorised contact.

We may also receive business contact information from publicly available business directories and professional platforms where appropriate for legitimate business communications.

4. How We Use Personal Information

We collect and use personal information for purposes connected with operating our business and providing our services.

These purposes include:

  • Responding to customer enquiries.
  • Preparing and providing quotations.
  • Assessing property conditions and service requirements.
  • Managing booking requests and confirming appointments.
  • Scheduling cleaning and gardening services.
  • Communicating changes to appointments and service arrangements.
  • Delivering the agreed cleaning and gardening services.
  • Contacting customers about access requirements and special instructions.
  • Preparing invoices, managing deposits and processing payments.
  • Responding to customer feedback, complaints and service concerns.
  • Maintaining customer, quotation and service records.
  • Managing our business operations and administration.
  • Improving our website, services and customer experience.
  • Protecting our website, systems, staff and customers against fraud, misuse and security risks.
  • Meeting applicable legal, regulatory, tax and insurance requirements.

We will not knowingly use personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or consent where required.

5. Emails and Business Communications

We may use email, telephone calls, SMS and other communication channels to communicate with customers.

This may include:

  • Enquiry acknowledgements.
  • Quotation correspondence.
  • Booking confirmations.
  • Appointment reminders.
  • Rescheduling and cancellation notifications.
  • Service updates.
  • Invoice and payment notifications.
  • Responses to customer feedback and complaints.
  • Other communications relating to requested services.

Customer and administrative communications may be processed or stored through email providers, website systems, business communication tools and other services used to operate our business.

6. How We Share Personal Information

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

We may share personal information where reasonably necessary to operate our business, provide our services or meet legal obligations.

This may include sharing information with:

  • Employees and authorised representatives involved in providing our services.
  • Contractors and subcontractors engaged to carry out requested work.
  • Website hosting and website management providers.
  • Email and communication service providers.
  • Booking, enquiry and customer management systems.
  • Information technology and website security providers.
  • Accounting, invoicing and business administration service providers.
  • Payment processing providers, where applicable.
  • Insurance providers and professional advisers.
  • Property managers or authorised representatives involved in arranging a service.
  • Government agencies, regulators, courts or law enforcement authorities where required or authorised by law.

We only seek to share information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant business purpose.

7. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website and business operations may use third-party service providers to support website hosting, website maintenance, enquiry forms, email communications, data storage, website security and other business functions.

Depending on the systems and features enabled, these providers may include website analytics services, advertising measurement tools, booking systems, embedded maps, payment services and other website technologies.

These third-party providers may collect, process or store personal information and technical information as necessary to provide their services. Their handling of information may also be subject to their respective privacy policies and service agreements.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of third-party services when using their features through our website.

8. Overseas Disclosure and Storage

Some third-party service providers engaged by our business may store or process personal information using systems or infrastructure located outside Australia.

As a result, personal information may be transferred to, stored in or accessible from other countries, depending on the providers and technologies used by our business.

Where the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and Australian Privacy Principles apply, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that any overseas disclosure or handling of personal information complies with our applicable legal obligations.

We will update this Privacy Policy if necessary when we identify relevant overseas recipients and data-handling arrangements.

9. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, improve user experience, maintain website security and understand website performance.

Cookies are small data files stored on your device when you visit a website.

Depending on the tools and features enabled on our website, cookies and similar technologies may be used for:

  • Essential website functionality and operation.
  • Website security and spam prevention.
  • Remembering certain website preferences.
  • Measuring website traffic and performance.
  • Understanding how visitors interact with website content.
  • Supporting third-party website features, analytics and advertising activities.

Third-party services, including embedded maps, analytics platforms or advertising tools, may also use cookies or similar technologies to collect technical information about website visits and interactions.

You can manage or disable cookies through your internet browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality or performance of parts of our website.

Where required by applicable law, we will provide appropriate information and choices regarding non-essential cookies and tracking technologies.

10. Storage and Security of Personal Information

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

Depending on the systems used, personal information may be stored electronically through website platforms, email systems, business software, cloud storage services and other authorised business systems.

Access to customer information is intended to be limited to people who reasonably require it for business operations or service delivery.

Although we take reasonable precautions, no website, electronic communication or data storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Customers should avoid sending highly sensitive information through ordinary website forms or unsecured communication channels.

11. Retention of Personal Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.

This may include retaining information to:

  • Complete and manage cleaning and gardening services.
  • Maintain customer and booking records.
  • Manage quotations, invoices and payments.
  • Meet tax, accounting and other legal obligations.
  • Manage insurance matters.
  • Resolve complaints and disputes.
  • Maintain appropriate business and security records.

When personal information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to securely destroy or de-identify it, unless we are required or permitted by law to retain it.

12. Access to and Correction of Personal Information

You may contact us to request access to personal information we hold about you.

You may also request that inaccurate, incomplete or outdated personal information be corrected.

When making a request, please provide sufficient information to help us identify the relevant records.

We may need to verify your identity before providing access or making changes to personal information.

We will consider and respond to requests within a reasonable period and in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

13. Privacy Complaints and Concerns

We take privacy concerns seriously.

If you believe your personal information has been collected, used, disclosed or handled inappropriately, please contact us.

When submitting a privacy complaint, please include:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • A description of your privacy concern.
  • Relevant dates or circumstances.
  • Any supporting information that may help us investigate the matter.

We will review the complaint and aim to respond within a reasonable period.

Where the Privacy Act 1988 applies and you are not satisfied with our response, you may be able to refer the matter to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Website: https://www.oaic.gov.au/

14. Photographs, Reviews and Testimonials

Photographs and documents supplied by customers are used primarily to assess properties, prepare quotations, plan services and manage the requested work.

We will not use identifiable customer photographs, personal property images, testimonials or project details for advertising, social media or other promotional purposes without appropriate permission.

Where a customer provides a review or testimonial for public use, we may publish it in accordance with the permission provided and applicable requirements.

Customers who wish to request the removal or review of published photographs or testimonials may contact us.

15. Direct Marketing and Promotional Communications

We may occasionally send information about our services, special offers, updates and other business promotions where we have the appropriate consent or other lawful basis.

Customers can opt out of promotional emails or messages at any time by:

  • Using the unsubscribe instructions included in the communication, where available; or
  • Contacting us at info@goldengrace.com.au

We will process marketing opt-out requests in accordance with applicable laws.

Please note that service-related communications, including quotation details, booking confirmations, appointment reminders and invoices, may still be sent where necessary to manage an existing enquiry or booking.

16. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, social media platforms or external services.

These websites are operated independently and may have their own privacy policies and information-handling practices.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

Customers should review the relevant privacy policies before providing personal information to third-party websites.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business, website systems, services or applicable privacy requirements.

Any updated version will be published on our website with a revised last-updated date.

We encourage customers to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we handle personal information.

18. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to request access to or correction of personal information, or want to raise a privacy concern, please contact us.
