About Us

Professional, Reliable, and Quality Service You Can Trust Across Perth, WA

Welcome to Golden Grace Cleaning And Gardening Solutions

Golden Grace Cleaning And Gardening Solutions is an Australian-registered, fully insured and GST-registered business with experience providing professional cleaning, gardening and property maintenance services.

We proudly provide services to residential and commercial clients across Perth and surrounding areas, Western Australia. We understand that every property and customer has different requirements, which is why we offer flexible and tailored solutions to suit a wide range of cleaning, gardening and property maintenance needs.

What We Do

We offer a comprehensive range of services designed to help maintain clean, hygienic and well-presented homes, workplaces and commercial properties.

Our services include:

  • Commercial and Office Cleaning: Regular office cleaning and cleaning for medical centres, childcare centres, schools, restaurants, cafés, gyms, retail spaces and other commercial facilities.
  • Residential Cleaning: General house cleaning, deep cleaning, end-of-lease cleaning and one-off cleaning services.
  • Specialist Cleaning: Carpet cleaning, internal and external window cleaning, post-construction cleaning, builders cleaning and Airbnb or short-term accommodation cleaning.
  • Gardening and Property Maintenance: Lawn mowing, garden maintenance, hedge trimming, weeding and green waste removal.
  • Additional Property Services: Rubbish removal, waste clearance, pressure washing, decluttering, downsizing and senior home transition support.

Whether you require regular cleaning and maintenance, a one-off service or additional support for a specific project, we aim to provide a solution that suits your requirements.

Our Approach

At Golden Grace, we understand that reliability, quality and clear communication are important when choosing a cleaning and property maintenance service provider.

We place importance on understanding our customers' requirements, providing suitable service arrangements and maintaining a consistent standard of work. We also value professional relationships with businesses and organisations, including opportunities to support their operations through subcontracting and ongoing service arrangements.

Our goal is to make cleaning and property maintenance easier for our customers by providing dependable services and practical solutions they can rely on.

Our Commitment

We are committed to providing professional, reliable and quality services while building positive, long-term relationships with our customers and business partners.

Whether it is a residential property, commercial facility, garden or larger maintenance project, we aim to approach every job with care, professionalism and attention to detail.
