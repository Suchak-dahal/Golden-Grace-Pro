Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our residential cleaning, commercial cleaning, pressure washing, and gardening services in Perth, WA.

▸ What cleaning and gardening services does Golden Grace offer?

We offer a comprehensive suite of property care services across Perth, WA. Our cleaning services include commercial and office cleaning, medical centre and childcare cleaning, residential house cleaning, deep cleaning, end-of-lease vacate bond cleans, carpet steam cleaning, internal/external window cleaning, builders/post-construction cleaning, and Airbnb turnovers. Our gardening services include regular lawn mowing, edging, hedge trimming, weeding, green waste removal, pruning, and high-pressure cleaning for driveways, patios, and outdoor hard surfaces.

▸ Which areas and suburbs in Perth do you service?

We service the entire Perth metropolitan area. Our coverage includes the Northern Suburbs (Joondalup, Wanneroo, Wangara, Hillarys, etc.), Perth CBD, Western and Inner Suburbs (Subiaco, Claremont, Nedlands, Cottesloe, Victoria Park), and Southern/Eastern Suburbs (Fremantle, Cockburn, Canning Vale, Armadale, Rockingham, Mandurah). Check our Service Areas page for a full suburb list.

▸ How can I get a free quote?

Getting a free quote is fast and straightforward. You can submit our online quote request form, call us directly on 0485 903 551, or email info@goldengrace.com.au. For residential cleaning, vacate cleans, and lawn mowing, we can usually provide a fast upfront estimate. For larger commercial facilities or specialized projects, we are happy to arrange a complimentary on-site assessment.

▸ Do you provide fixed prices or custom quotations?

We provide transparent, competitive custom quotes tailored specifically to the size of your property, its current condition, and your exact service requirements. Every quotation clearly outlines the inclusions so you know exactly what is covered before any work commences.

▸ Are there any hidden or additional fees?

No. Golden Grace operates with complete price transparency. All inclusions, GST (where applicable), and scope details are communicated upfront. If our team arrives on site and discovers significant extra requirements outside the agreed scope (such as heavy mould remediation, excessive rubbish removal, or hazardous waste), we will always discuss and confirm any additional pricing with you before proceeding.

▸ How do booking deposits and payments work?

For one-off services, end-of-lease cleaning, and initial bookings, a deposit or advance payment may be requested to secure your chosen date and time slot. Ongoing commercial and regular residential clients are invoiced on agreed terms (such as weekly, fortnightly, or monthly). We accept secure electronic bank transfers (EFT) and major credit/debit cards. Full payment terms are detailed in our Terms and Conditions.

▸ Can I change, reschedule, or cancel my booking?

Yes, we understand that plans can change. We ask for at least 24 to 48 hours' notice for any rescheduling or cancellation so we can adjust our team's schedule without penalty. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice may be subject to a late cancellation fee as outlined in our Terms and Conditions.

▸ Can I combine cleaning with pressure washing, window cleaning, or gardening in one visit?

Absolutely! One of Golden Grace's biggest advantages is that we provide both comprehensive cleaning and complete garden maintenance under one roof. You can easily bundle home or office cleaning with exterior pressure washing, window washing, and lawn mowing, saving you the hassle of coordinating multiple different contractors.

▸ Do you offer regular, ongoing commercial or residential cleaning services?

Yes. We provide flexible ongoing service schedules, including daily, weekly, fortnightly, or monthly visits for both residential homes and commercial workplaces (offices, medical clinics, retail stores, gyms, and communal strata facilities). Regular scheduled services also benefit from priority booking and consistent dedicated service personnel.

▸ Can you accommodate urgent or end-of-lease vacate cleaning requests?

Yes. We know end-of-lease handovers and property settlements often operate on tight deadlines. Subject to team availability, we strive to accommodate urgent, same-day, or next-day cleaning and garden tidy-up requests across Perth, WA. Contact us as early as possible to secure your preferred timeslot.

▸ Do you bring your own cleaning supplies, equipment, and gardening tools?

Yes, our teams arrive fully equipped with professional-grade cleaning chemicals, commercial vacuum cleaners, microfibre systems, high-pressure washers, commercial lawn mowers, line trimmers, hedge trimmers, and all required gardening tools. If you have specific preferences or proprietary products you would like us to use, simply let us know in advance.

▸ Are your products and services safe for pets and children?

Yes, safety is a core priority for us. We select eco-conscious, non-toxic, and biodegradable cleaning products wherever possible to ensure a healthy indoor environment that is completely safe for children, pets, and individuals with allergies or sensitivities.

▸ Do I need to be home during the cleaning or gardening appointment?

No, you do not need to be on site while we work, provided safe access to the property has been arranged in advance (such as a lockbox code, key collection, or someone granting entry upon arrival). Many of our residential and commercial clients provide key or code access. We secure your property thoroughly when the job is finished.

▸ Are your cleaners and gardeners police-cleared and fully insured?

Yes, 100%. All Golden Grace staff and contractors undergo strict background screening and national police checks. Furthermore, we carry comprehensive public liability insurance coverage ($10M+) and WorkCover protections, giving you complete peace of mind whenever our team is on your property.

▸ What if I am not satisfied with any part of the service?

Your satisfaction is our top commitment. If any area included in the agreed scope of work does not meet your expectations, please notify us within 24 hours of service completion with details and photos. We will promptly review the issue and arrange a complimentary revisit to rectify the specific area at no additional charge.

▸ What are your operating hours?

Our customer support and operational hours are Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays (with special arrangements available for select ongoing commercial contracts).
